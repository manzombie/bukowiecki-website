Research Studio / Research #02

Deeplight

Pilot a compact research submarine into flooded caves to recover the lost Halcyon's flight recorder. Your headlights are your eyes — and your only weapon.

Keyboard + mouse or touch · ~6–10 min expedition

Paused

Hull breached

Continuing costs 750 points and restores 70% hull.

Extracted

Score = pickups & kills × chain multiplier + extraction, hull and time bonuses.

Controls

Keyboard + mouse

W / SThrust forward / brake & reverse
A / DTurn left / right
Space / Ctrl or ZRise / descend
ShiftBoost (drains energy)
MouseAim headlights (limited gimbal, ±40°)
Left click (hold)Fire light pulses (energy)
Right click / QSonar ping
CChase / cockpit camera
EscPause & settings
RRestart (on the failure screen)

Touch

Left thumbStick: up = thrust, down = brake, sideways = turn
Right thumbDrag anywhere free to aim
▲ / ▼Rise / descend

SONAR · CAM · IITap

Reading the caves

  • ▣ Gold bands — salvage (score)
  • ✚ Green cross canister — hull repair
  • ◉ Cyan beacons — the safe passage
  • ◆ Red buoys, bones — danger, better loot
  • ✺ Spiked orbs — mines: blink faster as they arm
  • ◌ Shades — freeze while lit by your beams
