Dot Studio

Generative halftone dot fields, driven by a seed. Tune the maths, then export as a transparent PNG or SVG to drop straight into Canva. The artwork is dots on pure transparency, nothing baked behind.

What this is

Dot Studio generates the dotted, halftone artwork used across this 10-day sprint, the kind where dots of varying size form a shape on their own. Pick a pattern, tune the maths with the sliders, and the preview updates live on a transparency checkerboard so you see exactly what exports.

Four patterns

Phyllotaxis spiral (golden-angle sunflower with a centre void), Flow field (a grid bent along flowing curves), Radial burst (rings from the centre), and Wave field (a sine interference grid). The Edge fade control floats the shape so it never touches the frame.

Transparent export

Export a PNG at 1×/2×/4× or an SVG (vector), both on real transparency, no background baked in, ready to drop onto any colour in Canva.

Seeded & reproducible

Every image is built from a seed: the same seed plus the same settings always makes the identical image. Re-roll for a new one, or type your own. The seed is in the filename so any look is traceable.

Private

100% in your browser. No upload, no server, no tracking. Nothing you make leaves your machine.
