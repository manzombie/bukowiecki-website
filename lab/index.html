Research Studio
10 tools in 10 days

One generative-art tool a day, for ten days. Each one is a small machine for making whole collections — algorithmic, reproducible, and designed in the browser by anyone, no code required.

Client-side, no servers Deterministic by seed Open process

The Ten Days

Day 01 Live

Research #01 — Algorithm Studio

Design a generative algorithm with plain sliders, set collection rules, then generate and curate a full series. Every piece is reproducible from its seed.

Day 02 Live

Research #02 — Deeplight

A first-person submarine tunnel-runner. Hand-drawn level maps are auto-converted into 3D cave networks; steer the current, dodge, and surface.

Dive in →
Day 03 Live

Research #03 — Darts Coach

A webcam darts companion: it watches your throwing form and coaches the fundamentals, then pairs each dart's score to its posture. All on-device.

Open the coach →
Day 04 Live

Research #04 — Babcia Chat

A family chat across a language gap: everyone writes in their own language and reads the whole conversation in theirs, translated automatically. Installs on your phone, with notifications.

Open the chat →
Day 05 Live

Research #05 — Cheesecake Factory

Design a layered cheesecake with plain controls, watch it render in 3D in real time, and get an instant recipe to match. A tasty little configurator.

Start baking →
Day 06 Live

Research #06 — EDL Checker

A post-production delivery checker: point it at your uploads folder and see every package, which shots and versions were sent when. Reads locally in the browser — nothing is uploaded.

Open the checker →
Day 07 Live

Research #07 — ALE Merge Bench

Merge a vendor CSV into an Avid-exported bin ALE and get a re-importable ALE that fills the bin's columns — with every match shown to review first. Keys stay byte-exact. Reads locally; nothing uploaded.

Open the bench →
Day 08 Live

Research #08 — Restaurant Reviewer

Answer a few quick questions about a meal and get a fair, well-written review to paste into Google, plus a star rating that actually matches your words. Two minutes, mobile-first.

Write a review →
Day 09 Live

Research #09 — Dot Studio

A generative halftone dot-field studio: phyllotaxis, flow, radial and wave patterns from a seed, floated off the frame, exported as transparent PNG or SVG. The tool that makes this sprint's own artwork.

Make some dots →
Day 10 Live

Research #10 — Pain Board

The finale, built to outlive the sprint: a public, comments-first board where editorial, post and VFX pros share the workflow pains they live with, swap workarounds, and where solved ones point to a tool. A FRAME/SHIFT property.

Join the Pain Board ↗
