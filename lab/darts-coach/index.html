Darts Coach

Your webcam watches your throwing form and coaches the fundamentals — stance, stillness, follow-through, rhythm. Play a game or just practise. Everything runs on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Put the laptop on your front-left (for a right-hander), angled at your body. The placement helper will confirm it sees you.

Solo practice = posture feedback only, no score.
