ABOUT ME

VFX

I've been working in film and TV since 2007, shaping stories through visual effects on some of the most ambitious productions in modern cinema—Avatar, Gravity, Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows: Part 2, Everest, Paddington, and Alien: Covenant, among others.

Freelance

For the last seven years, I've worked as a freelance VFX Editor, collaborating with leading studios on high-profile streaming shows like Black Mirror, Dracula, The Witcher: Blood Origin, and Kaos.

Focus

My role has always been about making the invisible work—bridging creative vision with the technical realities of post-production.
